# Illustrated page background (optional)

The pages already use a vector watermark (`public/ui/watermark.svg`) over a light
parchment tint (`--page-background` in `app/globals.scss`). To try an illustrated
background instead, generate this image, save it as `public/ui/page-background.webp`
(about 200 KB) and point `--page-watermark` to it.

**page-background.webp** (2400×1200, landscape)

Very light, low contrast background illustration for a fantasy card game menu, almost
white parchment tones (ivory, cream, pale gold) with faint lavender and pale amber
highlights. A huge antique astrolabe and clockwork dial drawn as a delicate engraving
watermark, thin ink lines at very low opacity, brass gears, roman numerals and an
hourglass in the center, faint constellations and arcane circles around it. Soft
vignette, lots of empty space in the middle for interface panels, no strong shapes,
no characters. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark signature
